Water running right now?

Do this first, then call. We answer 24 hours a day.

Call (270) 442-3537
  1. Shut off the water. Turn the main shut-off valve clockwise. It's usually where the water line enters the house, or by the water heater.
  2. Keep away from electricity. If water is near outlets or appliances, stay out and switch off the breaker only if you can reach it from a dry spot.
  3. Open a faucet low in the house. It drains what's left in the pipes and takes pressure off the leak.
